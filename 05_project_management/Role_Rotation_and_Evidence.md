Replace placeholders after the team agrees. The assessment requires each document to state the member name, assigned role and date. Keep a weekly contribution log, meeting minutes, screenshots, issue links and commit IDs.

## Evidence checklist

- Sprint Planning invitation, agenda, attendance and decision log
- Product Goal, Product Backlog and Sprint Backlog with change history
- Weekly Scrum notes and increment screenshots
- Sprint Review agenda, demo recording/screenshot and stakeholder feedback
- Sprint Retrospective: what went well, what did not, actionable improvement
- Requirement/design versions and traceability matrix
- Test cases, test data, results and defect decisions
- Risk register, role rotation and job allocation
- AI-use statement and source citations for the final report
